A PROPOSED ROUTE

Ten days on
the pilgrim’s path.

A planning outline for a 10-day / 9-night Char Dham Yatra starting and ending in Haridwar. The actual route, overnight stops, travel times and temple access must be verified for your dates.

Kedarnath access
A vehicle does not drive to the temple. The onward journey from the applicable roadhead requires trekking or a separately arranged, authorised local transport option, subject to current rules and availability.

  1. 01
    Haridwar → Barkot area

    Road journey; overnight stop in the Barkot area, subject to confirmed accommodation and route.

  2. 02
    Yamunotri visit → Barkot area

    Travel to the applicable base point, continue to Yamunotri on foot or by an authorised local arrangement; return to the overnight area.

  3. 03
    Barkot area → Uttarkashi area

    Road journey and overnight stop; route and road conditions to be reconfirmed.

  4. 04
    Gangotri visit → Uttarkashi area

    Road journey to Gangotri for darshan, then return to the overnight area as planned.

  5. 05
    Uttarkashi area → Guptkashi area

    Long road-transfer day through mountain roads; planned rest breaks and overnight stop to be confirmed.

  6. 06
    Guptkashi area → Kedarnath base

    Transfer to the applicable roadhead. Continue to Kedarnath by trek or an authorised local transport arrangement; overnight plan is subject to availability.

  7. 07
    Kedarnath visit → Guptkashi area

    Darshan and return from Kedarnath according to the selected access arrangement, fitness and operating conditions.

  8. 08
    Guptkashi area → Badrinath area

    Road transfer and overnight stop; road access and local arrangements to be verified.

  9. 09
    Badrinath visit → Rudraprayag area

    Darshan and onward road journey to the planned overnight stop, subject to route and conditions.

  10. 10
    Rudraprayag area → Haridwar

    Return road journey to Haridwar; allow flexibility for mountain-road delays.

Illustrative planning outline only—not a confirmed itinerary. Distances, operating dates, temple timings, road status, accommodation, trek stages and local transport arrangements must be checked with the relevant authorities and suppliers before booking.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before

Ask us directly →
Where does the Char Dham Yatra start?+

Haridwar is the proposed departure point shown on this website. The operating address and exact pickup point are not yet confirmed; please verify them with the business before travel.

Does the vehicle take us directly to Kedarnath Temple?+

No. Road transport goes only as far as the applicable roadhead. Reaching the temple requires trekking or a separately arranged, authorised local transport option. Current rules, availability and suitability must be confirmed for your dates.

Are meals included in every package?+

Meal arrangements depend on the package and itinerary. A full-board proposal should specify breakfast, lunch and dinner, where applicable. Do not assume meals are included unless they are listed on your accepted written proposal.

Are the package prices on this website final?+

No rates are being advertised as confirmed offers. Request a written quotation for your dates and group; services and price are subject to review and explicit business confirmation.

When is my booking confirmed?+

Submitting an enquiry or opening WhatsApp does not confirm a booking. It is confirmed only after you accept the final written details and DivyaPath Travels confirms the booking with you.

Can you customise a family or group tour?+

You can request a customised family or group proposal. Routes, dates, accommodation, vehicle and meal arrangements will be discussed and confirmed before acceptance.
